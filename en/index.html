About

Good software doesn’t stop at the screen; it works on the device, in the field, and for the people who use it. That’s why, before I write any code, I always ask the same question: who will use this, where, and how? I turn the answer into solutions that bring design and engineering together.

I work in a research-driven, solution-oriented way: I first understand a problem thoroughly, then look for the simplest, most functional solution. I bring design knowledge, such as visual hierarchy and user experience, into every project. During my internships I developed device-control software and experienced machine reporting and ERP processes first-hand; in our team project we built quantum key distribution on a Raspberry Pi. I am always open to learning and new experiences.
