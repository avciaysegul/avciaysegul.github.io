About me

During my internship at MAY Fren Sistemleri, I worked on improving the existing device control screen. I observed the operators in the field to identify their needs and pinpointed the shortcomings of the first version. Based on these findings, I made the interface clearer and more modern. With the new design, even a user with no prior knowledge of the device can use the screen with ease.

I study Software Engineering at Kırklareli University, and I enjoy designing interfaces. I am also interested in quantum technologies: my team and I presented the quantum key distribution system we built on a Raspberry Pi as a paper at SAVTEK 2026, and I am continuing to work on this project. I am open to new projects and different experiences.

An interactive recipe app with a Turkish and English interface that asks questions while cooking and adapts quantities and steps to the answers.

Approach I built this small prototype during my internship at MAY Fren Sistemleri to learn how to separate Lua scenarios from the interface. Machines also work step by step, like cooking recipes, so I built the study around that comparison. I wrote the flows of 22 recipes as Lua scenarios and ran them with a Lua engine embedded on the Rust side; the interface only shows the questions and steps the scenario asks.
